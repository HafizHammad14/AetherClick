### 1. Overview
Hammad Designz ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage for the **AetherClick - Auto Clicker** desktop application available on the Microsoft Store.

### 2. Zero Data Collection
**AetherClick - Auto Clicker does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage telemetry, or sensitive user information.**

Specifically:
- **No Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, location data, or device identifiers.
- **No Keystroke / Input Logging:** While AetherClick requires access to mouse and keyboard events to perform user-configured clicks and listen for hotkeys (e.g., F6 to toggle clicking), **no keystrokes or mouse activities are ever logged, recorded, or transmitted**.
- **No Network Transmission:** The application operates 100% locally and offline on your computer. It does not communicate with external servers, telemetry endpoints, or cloud databases.

### 3. Application Permissions
AetherClick requests Windows desktop automation capabilities solely to fulfill its core function:
- **Simulating Mouse Clicks:** Automating left, right, middle, or double clicks based on the interval and coordinates configured by the user.
- **Global Hotkey Detection:** Registering custom keyboard shortcut hotkeys (such as F6) to start and stop the clicker without needing the window to be in the foreground.
- **Local Settings Storage:** User preferences (click interval, click type, hotkeys) are saved strictly locally on your PC.

### 4. Third-Party Services
AetherClick does not include third-party tracking libraries, analytics frameworks, advertising networks, or data brokers.

### 5. Children's Privacy
Because AetherClick collects zero personal data, it is safe for users of all ages and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.

### 6. Security
Your privacy is fundamentally guaranteed by our architecture: all computation and simulation remain isolated on your local operating system.
